The design

Every screen in its shipping state, at true 390 × 844. This file is the spec — no alternatives, no debate. The options I considered and why I rejected them live in decisions.html; the tokens live in tokens.css; the build order lives in REVAMP_PLAN.md.

Numbers reconcile across every screen: accounts sum to Out, categories sum to Out, donut arcs are true percentages, and the pace tick is 11/31 of the month.

01 · Palette

Three ink levels, three surfaces, one hairline. Green means money arriving or kept; red means over budget; everything else is ink. Category hues are generated in OKLCH at fixed lightness and chroma so a donut reads as one family — and the 150–185 band is skipped because it belongs to positive. Every text colour clears WCAG AA on all three surfaces.

02 · Type

Geist throughout, Geist Mono on the keypad. Nothing above weight 600 — size carries the hierarchy. font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums on body, input and button, so a column of amounts can never drift.

03 · Components

Everything the screens are built from.

04 · Home

Greeting and full date left, the figure centred, the pace bar full width — its fill is budget consumed, its tick is where even spending would put you. Five blocks, all answering am I OK today.

05 · Transactions

Account activity sits above the search and filter row — it summarises the whole period, so it belongs above the controls that narrow it. Entries group by day with a running subtotal. Expense amounts are ink, not red.

06 · Tracker & Stats

Tracker is this period. Stats is every month, always calendar months. All months links both ways.
